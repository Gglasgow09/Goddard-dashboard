About This Dashboard

What We're Measuring & Why

Goddard Riverside collects end-of-year program data to answer three core questions about every program it operates. This dashboard brings those answers together in one place.

01

What are programs producing?

Tracking outputs like case management sessions, housing placements, meals served, and mental health services tells us whether programs are delivering on their core mission.

02

Who are they serving?

Demographic data across race, gender, age, income, and health status ensures programs are reaching the populations they were designed to serve equitably.

03

Are they operationally healthy?

Staffing capacity data — including FTE counts, vacant positions, and participants per staff member — reveals whether programs have the resources to sustain their work.

Data Quality

Flags & Notes from Data Cleaning

The following flags were identified during the cleaning process and should be reviewed before the next survey cycle.

⚠️

Corner House — Disability Count Exceeds Participants

36 participants reported with any disability but total unduplicated participants is 31. This may reflect participants with multiple disabilities being counted more than once. Recommend clarifying counting methodology.

⚠️

ACT Team — Income Data Not Collected

ACT Team does not collect participant income data. All income fields recorded as "Not Collected." Consider whether income tracking is feasible for this program type in future cycles.

⚠️

ACT Team — Staff FTE Rounded

Raw data reported 9.5 full-time staff members, suggesting one position is shared between programs at 50%. Rounded up to 10 for headcount reporting. Original value preserved in Raw Data tab.

⚠️

#DegreesNYC — High Staff Workload

At 21.3 participants per FTE, #DegreesNYC carries significantly more load per staff member than Corner House (7.75) and ACT Team (7.83). Leadership should assess whether current staffing is sustainable.

✅

All Programs — Age Totals Match

Age group subtotals match total unduplicated participant counts for all three programs. No discrepancies found.

✅

All Programs — Gender Totals Match

Gender subtotals match total unduplicated participant counts for all three programs. No discrepancies found.
